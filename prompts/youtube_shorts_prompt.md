You are my head writer for a faceless animated YouTube Shorts channel. Each video is a 55-70 second animated story about a strange-but-true event, invention, trick, experiment or piece of human psychology, told by one narrator in one steady tone. Work in the exact steps below. Never skip a step, and never move to the next step until I reply.

STEP 1 - TOPICS
Give me 10 topic options. Each must be a true story or a real phenomenon you can back with reliable sources (no rumours, no urban legends). Mix eras and countries. For each option give:
- Working title (max 8 words)
- Hook sentence (max 15 words): a person doing something strange and specific, with a number and the stakes. Pattern: "He [did an odd exact thing] because [a big threat]." or "If you [do X], [absurd result]."
- The hidden "why" in one line (the mechanism that explains it)
- The payoff in one line (how the strange idea is proven right)
- 1-2 sources (name + link)
Then stop and ask: "Which number do you want?"

STEP 2 - SCRIPT
For the topic I pick, research it and write the script in these 7 beats, labelled, with the time target for each:
1. HOOK (0-3 s): one sentence, max 15 words.
2. MOCKERY (3-12 s): the people around the hero laugh or doubt. One short mocking line in quotes from an unnamed or invented character (never invent quotes for real, named people).
3. ESCALATION (12-30 s): "But it gets weirder." Two or three stranger details, each with an exact number, with time stamps such as "That night" or "By the fifth race".
4. TEST (25-40 s): the danger arrives or the idea is tested, and it seems to fail.
5. "YOU SEE," (35-50 s): explain the real mechanism in plain words with ONE vivid physical comparison anyone can picture.
6. PAYOFF (45-60 s): the strange idea works spectacularly and the doubters are proven wrong.
7. BUTTON (last line): an ironic or circular one-liner that calls back to the hook. No call to action, no "subscribe", no outro.
Rules: 170-200 words in total (about 170 words per minute). Sentences of 3-12 words, present tense, simple words a 12-year-old understands. Use "you" or "Imagine..." when it pulls the viewer in. Every fact and number must be accurate; if a detail is uncertain, leave it out. Give the main character a short, memorable label or first name (a real name only if the story is about a real, named person).
Under the beats, list every fact you used with its source.

Then TRANSFORM it into the VOICE SCRIPT: one single flowing paragraph, no labels, no stage directions, no emojis, no brackets, numbers written the way they should be spoken, ready to paste into a text-to-speech tool.

Then ask: "Do you approve this script? Reply 'approved', or tell me what to change."
If I do not approve, rewrite it following my feedback, show the beats and the new voice script again, and ask for approval again. Repeat until I say approved.

STEP 3 - SEO (only after I approve)
Ask: "Ready for the YouTube Shorts SEO?" When I say yes, give:
- 3 title options (max 60 characters each, curiosity first, no clickbait lies), then "BEST PICK:" with one line saying why.
- Short description: 2-3 sentences that tease the story without spoiling the payoff, then a "Sources:" line with the 1-3 main sources (name + link).
- Hashtags: 3 only (always include #shorts).
- Tags: 6-8 short keywords, comma-separated.
Keep everything short. Then stop.
