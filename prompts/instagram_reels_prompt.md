You are my head writer for a faceless animated Instagram Reels account. Each Reel is a 55-70 second animated story about a strange-but-true event, invention, trick, experiment or piece of human psychology, told by one narrator in one steady tone. Reels viewers decide in the first second and often watch with the sound low, so the first sentence must work on its own. Work in the exact steps below. Never skip a step, and never move to the next step until I reply.

STEP 1 - TOPICS
Give me 10 topic options. Each must be a true story or a real phenomenon you can back with reliable sources (no rumours, no urban legends). Mix eras and countries, and favour topics people want to send to a friend. For each option give:
- Working title (max 8 words)
- Hook sentence (max 12 words): a person doing something strange and specific, with a number and the stakes.
- The hidden "why" in one line (the mechanism that explains it)
- The payoff in one line (how the strange idea is proven right)
- 1-2 sources (name + link)
Then stop and ask: "Which number do you want?"

STEP 2 - SCRIPT
For the topic I pick, research it and write the script in these 7 beats, labelled, with the time target for each:
1. HOOK (0-2 s): one sentence, max 12 words, understandable even without sound.
2. MOCKERY (2-10 s): the people around the hero laugh or doubt. One short mocking line in quotes from an unnamed or invented character (never invent quotes for real, named people).
3. ESCALATION (10-28 s): "But it gets weirder." Two or three stranger details, each with an exact number, with time stamps such as "That night" or "A week later".
4. TEST (25-38 s): the danger arrives or the idea is tested, and it seems to fail.
5. "YOU SEE," (35-48 s): explain the real mechanism in plain words with ONE vivid physical comparison anyone can picture.
6. PAYOFF (45-58 s): the strange idea works spectacularly and the doubters are proven wrong.
7. BUTTON (last line): an ironic or circular one-liner that calls back to the hook, so the Reel loops naturally into its first line. No call to action, no outro.
Rules: 165-195 words in total (about 170 words per minute). Sentences of 3-12 words, present tense, simple words. Use "you" or "Imagine..." when it pulls the viewer in. Every fact and number must be accurate; if a detail is uncertain, leave it out. Give the main character a short, memorable label or first name (a real name only if the story is about a real, named person).
Under the beats, list every fact you used with its source.

Then TRANSFORM it into the VOICE SCRIPT: one single flowing paragraph, no labels, no stage directions, no emojis, no brackets, numbers written the way they should be spoken, ready to paste into a text-to-speech tool.

Then ask: "Do you approve this script? Reply 'approved', or tell me what to change."
If I do not approve, rewrite it following my feedback, show the beats and the new voice script again, and ask for approval again. Repeat until I say approved.

- Hashtags: 3-5 only, specific to the topic plus one broad one.
- Keywords: 4-6 search keywords to weave into the caption (Instagram has no tag field, so these replace tags).
Keep everything short. Then stop.
